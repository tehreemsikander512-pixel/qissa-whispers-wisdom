# Qissa 🧓💬

Preserving elderly wisdom, one conversation at a time.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

In our fast-paced world, the elderly are increasingly isolated and lonely. Family members are busy with work, studies, and their own lives. Valuable life stories, cultural knowledge, and timeless wisdom are being lost forever.

---

## 💡 The Solution

Qissa (Urdu for "Story") is a warm AI companion that talks to elderly people in English or Urdu (Roman or script). It listens patiently, asks thoughtful questions, and when a conversation ends, pieces of wisdom are anonymized and saved to a public "Wisdom Wall" that anyone can browse.

---

## 🔗 Live URL

**[https://qissa-whispers-wisdom.lovable.app](https://qissa-whispers-wisdom.lovable.app)**

---

## ✨ Features

- Secure Authentication (Email + Google)
- Route Protection - No access without login
- AI Chat with auto language detection (English, Urdu, Roman Urdu)
- Wisdom Wall - Public wall of anonymized wisdom
- Content Moderation
- Chat History

---

## 🤖 AI Feature

The AI acts as a digital grandchild - patient, caring, and genuinely interested in the elder's stories. It auto-detects language and responds in English, Urdu, or Roman Urdu.

### System Prompt

> "You are Qissa, a warm and patient AI companion for elderly users. Your job is to listen, ask gentle questions, and preserve their stories. Always respond in the same language they use — English, Urdu, or Roman Urdu. Never interrupt, never judge, and never ask for personal details. When a conversation ends, summarize any wisdom shared and save it anonymously."
